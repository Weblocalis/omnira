1. About Omnira and this policy

Omnira - Your World of Radio. (“Omnira”, “the app”, “we”) is a player that lets you search, filter and listen to internet radio stations operated by third parties. Omnira is not a radio station and does not broadcast its own programming. The stations, their audio streams and their logos belong to their respective operators.

This policy applies to the Omnira web app at [WEBSITE URL] and, where it is offered, to the Omnira Android app, which displays the same web app. It explains what stays on your device, what is exchanged with third parties when you use Omnira, and the choices you have.

Publisher and privacy contact: [EDITOR NAME], [BUSINESS ADDRESS], [CONTACT EMAIL]. See also the Legal Notice.

2. The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no payment and no contact form.
  • No advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools in Omnira’s own code.
  • Omnira has no server of its own that receives your information: the app’s code does not send your favorites, listening history or settings to us or to anyone else.
  • Your favorites, history and preferences are saved on your device only, and you can delete them at any time.
  • As with any web page or app, using Omnira makes your device connect to third-party servers (radio streams, a content delivery network, an icon service and our hosting provider). They can see technical information such as your IP address. See section 4.

3. Information stored on your device

Omnira saves the following items in your browser’s local storage or cache so that the features you use keep working. They are not sent to us, and they contain no name, email address or account identifier.

These items stay on your device until you delete them: use the Clear button for the history, tap the heart again to remove a favorite, or clear the site data (or uninstall the app) in your browser or device settings. Anyone who uses the same browser profile on the same device can see these items.

If your browser blocks local storage, Omnira keeps working but cannot remember your choices after you close it.

Omnira itself has no server and receives none of the information below. However, to work, your device connects directly to the following third parties. Each of them can receive the technical information that any internet connection involves: your IP address, the type of browser or device, the time and the address requested. Their own privacy policies apply.

We do not control these third parties and cannot describe their cookies or retention periods. Third-party servers may set their own cookies when your device contacts them.

5. What Omnira does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or collect your name, email address or payment details.
  • It does not show advertising and does not include advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking software.
  • It does not ask for access to your location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos or notifications.
  • It does not set cookies itself.
  • It does not sell your personal information or use it for profiling.

If this changes (for example if analytics or advertising were added), this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

6. Purposes, legal bases and retention

Because Omnira has no server that receives your favorites, history or settings, we do not hold this information. It is saved on your device only to provide the features you ask for, and it is kept until you delete it.

Where data protection law such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to the limited technical data handled when the app is delivered by our hosting provider, the purpose is to deliver and secure the service (legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention of that data is governed by the provider’s own practices. The third parties in section 4 process data under their own responsibility and policies.

7. Security

Omnira is delivered over HTTPS and the third-party style and script files it loads are verified with integrity hashes. Most stations stream over HTTPS, but a few may use unencrypted HTTP, which your browser may block on a secure page. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and choices

Choices. You can delete everything Omnira saves on your device (section 3), choose not to play a station, or stop using the app at any time.

Rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority (in France, the CNIL). Because Omnira does not hold your listening data, we may have nothing to provide, correct or delete, but you can write to us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with any question about your privacy. To exercise rights regarding data held by the third parties in section 4, contact them directly.

9. Children

Omnira is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The content of radio stations is controlled by their operators and may not be suitable for children; parents and guardians should supervise how children use the app.

10. International transfers

The third-party servers in section 4 may be located in other countries than yours, including outside the European Economic Area. Their transfer safeguards are described in their own policies.
